# Security

No stable public release or security response SLA exists. Current scope is the private0.1.0-rc.1 candidate and recorded local tests. Report vulnerabilities privately through the channel used to obtain this candidate until the owner configures a verified reporting route. Do not disclose exploit details or raw sessions in public issues.

Maintainers may investigate/fix at their discretion without deadlines. Use synthetic authorized targets, minimize evidence and review images/traces before sharing. [Threat model](docs/SECURITY.md). License/third-party rights and release authorization remain unresolved.
